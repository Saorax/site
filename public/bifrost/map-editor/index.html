In this map

Removing art doesn't touch floors, spawns or bot paths. Ctrl+Z undoes it. To remove one piece, click it on the map and press Delete.

Place art

To see the game's art, click Game art folder… at the top and pick the mapArt folder in your Brawlhalla folder. Nothing is uploaded. Choose what a click does: place it as art (with an art piece selected it swaps its picture, otherwise it's placed in the middle of the view) or make it the background. Any picture can be the background, yours too. Art is only looks; add a floor where players should stand.
